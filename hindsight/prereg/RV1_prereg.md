# RV-1: review-response analyses for paper 1 (zero new model calls)

Frozen 2026-09-24 before any RV-1 analysis was run on a preregistered model's arms. Trigger: a simulated
AAAI review of paper 1 (main_submission.pdf) whose central objection is that D-M and W identify
date-conditioned period knowledge, not knowledge available only after the decision date, and whose
inference objections are that the headline interval omits replicate variance and treats 11 crisis months
as exchangeable although they form 3 episodes. All analyses reuse frozen FM-1/bench arms.

State at freeze: the three scripts were run only on a model outside this document
(llama-3.1-70b-awq) to test mechanics, and the primary model's published E2 point estimate (+25.6) was
recomputed as a check. No pre-shock, anachronism or hierarchical number exists for the models below.

## Models
- **Primary**: Gemini 2.5 Flash, `outputs/fm1/gemini-2.5-flash` (R/D/M/W, 3 replicates, 240 dates).
- **Secondary** (paper 1's causal core): Gemini 2.5 Pro (`outputs/bench/gemini-2.5-pro`, D/M 3 reps, R/W 1),
  DeepSeek v4-flash (`outputs/bench/deepseek-v4-flash`, 2 reps). Only the 240 panel dates are used.

## A. Pre-shock test (reviewer Q1: is any of the date effect knowledge available only after t?)
Dates: `prereg/RV1_preshock_dates.json`, generated by `scripts/make_rv1_preshock_dates.py` from S&P 500
closes and daily VIX only. A decision date is **calm-looking** if its trailing ~3-month return is >= 0 and
its last VIX close on or before t is <= the 2005-2023 daily median (16.88). **SHOCK** = calm-looking and the
next ~3 months' drawdown <= -10% (8 dates: 2008-05, 2010-04, 2015-06, 2015-07, 2018-09, 2019-12, 2020-01,
2020-02; about 5 episodes); **CONTROL** = calm-looking and drawdown > -5% (72 dates). Dates <= 2023-12,
crisis set excluded. Sensitivity sets (-7%/-4%, 42 days; -15%/-5%, 84 days) are reported, never primary.
Disclosed: before this rule was written, the chat proposed a hand list (2007-07, 2008-08, 2011-07, 2015-07,
2018-09, 2020-01, 2020-02, 2021-12); the rule, not that list, defines the sets, and the list is not analysed.

Estimand: **PS = Gap_D(S,C) - Gap_M(S,C)**, the E2 form with SHOCK/CONTROL in place of crisis/calm
(bearish sketch shares, replicates pooled). At a SHOCK date the tape is calm, so a date-specific bearish
shift that the masked arm does not show comes either from knowledge of what followed t or from coeval
non-market news the tape had not priced (e.g. the first Wuhan reports before 2020-01-15, lingering credit
stress in 2008-05). PS therefore bounds the future-only component from above rather than isolating it; the
anachronism audit (B) is the instrument that can separate the two, and the paper reports PS per date.
Inference: date-level bootstrap (B = 10,000, seed 2026), plus per replicate and replicate x date.
**Readings (primary model, primary set, date-level CI):** DETECTED if the CI lies above 0; NEGATIVE* if
below 0; BOUNDED if it includes 0 and its upper end is below +10pp (a future-only component under ~40% of
E2's +25.6pp); INCONCLUSIVE otherwise. Secondary models and sensitivity sets are reported with the same
labels and do not change the primary reading.

## B. Anachronism audit (reviewer item 13: temporally impossible content)
Lexicon: `prereg/RV1_lexicon.json`, 19 named events with first-public dates (ambiguous generic words
excluded). A **hit** is a raw response at decision date t (R, D or M arm; W is audited elsewhere) that
contains a term first public after t. Every hit is then classified by hand, and the classification is
listed hit by hit with its context:
- **assertion**: the text refers to the event as having happened or as happening;
- **hypothetical**: conditional, generic or forward-looking use that does not presuppose the event;
- **false match**: the regex matched something else.
Primary number: responses with >= 1 assertion, D arm vs M arm, primary model; two-sided Fisher exact test.
Reading: any D-arm assertion is reported as temporally impossible content in dated generations; zero
D-arm assertions are reported with the rule-of-three bound (< 3/n responses at 95%).

## C. Hierarchical inference (reviewer Q2, Q3)
E1, E2 (all three models) and E3 (primary) under four resampling schemes, B = 10,000, seed 2026:
date (the paper's), replicate x date, episode-block (resample the 3 crisis episodes and 3 calm years, then
dates within), replicate x episode-block. These are reported intervals, not tests; no reading is attached.
